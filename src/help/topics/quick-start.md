Chalkline turns ideas into diagrams: add shapes, connect them, then style and share.

## Your first diagram

1. Add a shape: tap one in the palette, or drag it onto the canvas. On a phone, tap **+** first.
2. Add a second shape the same way.
3. Connect them: switch to **Link** mode, tap the first shape, then the second.
4. Change a label: double-click a shape, or select it and type in the **Label** field.
5. Your work saves in this browser as you go. Use **Save as JSON** in the menu to keep a copy.

> Not sure where to begin? Choose **Start from a template** or **Load an example** on the empty canvas.

## The three modes

The mode switch sits in the toolbar.

- **Select** (`V`): tap to select, drag to move, drag on empty canvas to draw a selection box.
- **Pan** (`H`): any drag moves the view. Handy on touch screens, so you never move a shape by accident.
- **Link** (`L`): tap a source shape, then a target, to connect them. Tap the source again to cancel.

## Where things are

- **Palette**: shapes and stencils. On a phone it opens from **+**.
- **Properties**: appear when something is selected. Style, notes, locking and more.
- **Menu** (the three lines): new, open, save, export and example diagrams.
- **More room**: on desktop, the chevron buttons collapse the palette and the right panel to slim rails.
