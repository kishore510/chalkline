---
title: Locking
order: 7
keywords: lock, locked, unlock, protect, freeze
---
Lock things you don't want to move by accident, like a background frame or a finished section.

- Select a shape, group or lane and turn on **Locked** in its properties.
- A locked item can't be moved, resized or deleted. Its label and notes stay editable.
- Locking a group locks everything inside it.
- Align, distribute and auto-arrange skip locked items and tell you how many they skipped.

To lock a whole layer, use the padlock in [Layers](help:layers).
