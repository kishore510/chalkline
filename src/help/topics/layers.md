Layers let you split a diagram, for example a base architecture with an overlay for security zones.

- Open **Layers** from the toolbar (on desktop, it's a tab beside Properties).
- **Add layer** creates a new layer on top and makes it active. New shapes go on the **active** layer.
- Tap the eye to **hide** a layer, or the padlock to **lock** it.
- **Show only this layer** and **Show all** are quick ways to focus.
- Move a selection to another layer with the **Layer** field in its properties.
- Deleting a layer lets you move its items elsewhere or delete them with it.

The toolbar shows a small badge when layers are hidden or locked, so nothing goes missing unnoticed.

> Exports leave out hidden layers unless you turn on **Include hidden layers** in the menu.
