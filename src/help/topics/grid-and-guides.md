Open **View** in the toolbar (on a phone, it's in the menu).

- **Snap to grid** (`G`): shapes jump to grid positions as you move or resize them.
- **Smart guides**: while dragging or resizing, lines appear when edges or centres line up, when gaps are equal, or when sizes match. Guides win over the grid. Hold `Alt` to pause them for a moment.
- **Grid display**: dots, lines or off.

These settings are remembered in this browser.
