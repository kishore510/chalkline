Open **View** in the toolbar (on a phone, it's in the menu).

- **Snap to grid** (`G`): shapes jump to grid positions as you move or resize them.
- **Smart guides**: while dragging or resizing, lines appear when edges or centres line up, when gaps are equal, or when sizes match. Guides win over the grid. Hold `Alt` to pause them for a moment.
- **Grid display**: dots, lines or off.

## Nudging with the arrow keys

With a keyboard, the arrow keys move the selection: one grid square when snap is on (one pixel when it's off). Hold `Shift` for a bigger step: five squares, or 10 pixels.

Nudges follow the same smart guides and grid as dragging, so a shape lands in the same place either way, and an arrow key only moves it one way. Holding a key down, or pressing it quickly, counts as one step for undo. Locked shapes, shapes on locked layers and shapes in locked groups stay put. Moving a group moves everything inside it.

These settings are remembered in this browser.
