Connectors join two shapes and follow them when they move.

## Draw a connector

- **Link mode** (works everywhere, best on touch): choose **Link** in the toolbar, tap the source shape, then the target. Tap the source again to cancel.
- **With a mouse** in Select mode: hover a shape to show its edge dots, then drag from a dot onto another shape.

## Auto and pinned sides

Each end of a connector attaches to a side of its shape.

- **Auto** (the default): the end picks the nearest side as shapes move, and steers around other shapes.
- **Pinned**: the end stays on the side you chose.

To pin an end, select the connector and choose **Start side** or **End side** in the properties. **Reset to auto** puts both ends back.

## Docking points

Select a connector to show a round grip at each end. Drag a grip to another docking point on the same shape, or onto a different shape, to move that end there. Tap a grip for **Reset to auto**.

## Arrowheads, lines and labels

In the connector properties:

- **Label**: short text drawn on the line, for example "reads" or "HTTPS". You can also double-click the connector.
- **Line**: straight, right angles, rounded corners or curved; dashed; colour and width.
- **Start** and **End**: no arrowhead, an open arrow or a filled arrow.

> Connectors sharing a side can be spread out with **Tidy connectors**. See [Tidy and Auto-arrange](help:tidy-and-arrange).
