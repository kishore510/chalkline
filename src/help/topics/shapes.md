## Add a shape

- **Tap** a shape in the palette to add it in the middle of the view.
- **Drag** it onto the canvas to place it exactly. On a phone, press and hold a shape in the drawer, then drag.
- Search the palette by name, or pick from **Recently used**.

## Edit a label

- Double-click the shape, or select it and press `Enter`.
- Or type in the **Label** field in the properties.
- On touch, long-press the shape and choose **Edit label**.

Labels wrap to fit, and the shape grows if the text needs more room.

## Move, resize and select

- Drag a shape to move it (in Select mode).
- Drag the small square handles on a selected shape to resize it.
- To select several: drag a box around them on empty canvas, or hold `Shift` and click.
- `Ctrl A` selects everything.

## Duplicate, copy and delete

- **Duplicate**: the button at the top of the properties, the long-press menu, or `Ctrl D`.
- **Copy and paste**: `Ctrl C` and `Ctrl V`, even between browser tabs. On touch, copy from the long-press menu, then long-press empty canvas and choose **Paste here**.
- **Delete**: the bin button, or `Delete`. A message offers **Undo** straight away.

## Change shape

Select one or more shapes and use **Change shape** in the properties. The label, size and style stay.

See every available shape in the [Shapes reference](help:shapes-reference).
