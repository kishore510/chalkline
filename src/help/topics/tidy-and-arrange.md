The **Tidy** button (wand) cleans up a diagram in one go. It works in Select mode.

## Auto-arrange

Lays shapes out in rows by how they connect.

- **Direction**: left to right, or top to bottom.
- **Spacing**: compact, normal or roomy.
- Select some shapes first to arrange only those; otherwise the whole diagram is arranged.

Swimlane pools and locked items stay where they are. Your choices are remembered, and you can undo the result.

## Tidy connectors

Spreads out connectors that share a side of a shape, so they don't overlap. Turn on **Also clear pinned sides** to put pinned ends back on auto first.
