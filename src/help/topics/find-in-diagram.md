Find shapes by the words in their labels or notes. Capital letters and accents don't matter.

- **Open it**: the magnifying glass in the top bar, or `Ctrl F` (`Cmd F` on a Mac) while the canvas has focus. On a phone, open the menu and choose **Find in diagram**.
- **Move between matches**: the up and down arrows next to the box, or `Enter` and `Shift Enter` on a keyboard. The view moves to centre each one. On a phone, you can also tap a match in the list.
- **What you see**: every match gets a soft highlight, and the current one has a strong outline. Shapes whose label matches come first, then shapes that match only in their notes, each from top to bottom.
- **Clear and close**: the × in the box clears it. **Close** (or `Escape`) closes search and removes the highlight.

## Hidden layers and collapsed groups

- Matches on hidden layers aren't shown. Search says how many there are, and **Show** makes those layers visible again.
- A match inside a collapsed group highlights the group. **Expand** opens it, as one undo step.

> Search never changes your diagram, and the highlight isn't saved or exported.
