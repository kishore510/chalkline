Select two or more shapes to line them up.

- **On desktop** a bar appears at the top of the canvas.
- **On a phone or tablet** the buttons are in the properties.

## What each does

- **Align**: left, centre or right edges; top, middle or bottom edges.
- **Distribute** (three or more shapes): equal gaps across or down.
- **Match size**: the widest width, the tallest height, or both.

Each action is one undo step. Connectors are ignored, and locked shapes stay put.

> For lining up while you drag, turn on smart guides. See [Grid, snap and guides](help:grid-and-guides).
